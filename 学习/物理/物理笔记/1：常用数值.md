### 常用换算

1. 长度 $1\text{ km} = 10^{3} \text{ m} = 10^{6}  \text{ mm} = 10^{9} \mu \text{m} = 10^{12} \text{ nm}$
2. 时间 $1\text{ h} = 60 \text{ min} = 60^{2}\text{ s}$
3. 时间 $1 \text{ s} = 10^{3} \text{ ms} = 10^{6} \mu \text{s}$
4. 速度 $\text{1 m/s =  3.6 km/h} \approx \frac{1}{0.278} \text{ km/h}$
5. 质量 $1\text{ t} = 10^{3}\text{ kg} = 10^{6}\text{ g} = 10^{9}\text{ mg}$
6. 角度 $1^{\circ} = \frac{\pi}{180} \text{ rad}  \approx \frac{1}{57.3} \text{ rad}\approx 1.74 \times 10^{-2} \text{ rad}$
7. 质能换算 $1 \text{ u} \times \text{c}^{2} \approx 931.5 \text{ MeV}$
8. 功能换算 $1 \text{ MeV} = 10^{6} \text{ eV} = 1.6 \times 10^{-13} \text{ J}$
